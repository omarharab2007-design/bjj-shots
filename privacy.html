Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

BJJ Shots films and photographs jiu-jitsu competitions and athletes in the Gulf. This page explains what happens to your information on this website.

Booking enquiries

The booking form doesn’t send anything through this website. When you press send, WhatsApp or your own email app opens with a message to us, filled in from the form: your name, how to reach you, the kind of shoot, the event and, for athletes, your division and team. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

Photo orders

When you pick photos in the gallery, the list of photo IDs and an order code are saved in your own browser so your picks are still there when you come back. They never leave your device until you send the order to us on WhatsApp or by email. Clear them any time with “Clear order”. If you pay online, the payment provider handles your card details under its own privacy policy; we only see the payment and your order code.

What we keep

We keep your emails and messages to reply to you, plan your shoot and send your photos or video. We don’t sell them or add you to a mailing list. To have them deleted, email info@bjjshots.net.

Photos and video of you

If you appear in our footage and would like a clip or photo taken down from this site or our social accounts, email us and we’ll remove it.

No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising pixels and no cookies. The only thing it stores in your browser is your photo picks, described above.

Services this site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, like any web host.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and WhatsApp take you to those services, under their own privacy policies.
